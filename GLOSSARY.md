# Spartial clinical access

Terms distinguish routine care access from emergency access and the registry work needed to arrange care.

## Language

**Care assignment**:
An attributable, time-bounded responsibility for a staff member to participate in a patient's care at a facility.
_Avoid_: Unrestricted access, ownership

**Exceptional access grant**:
A reasoned, temporary emergency authorization for an eligible staff member, requiring independent review.
_Avoid_: Permanent bypass, administrator override

**Registry access**:
Reception's access to demographics needed to register patients and coordinate their care.
_Avoid_: Clinical access

**Privacy review**:
A review of sensitive access by an authorized person who did not request the exceptional access being reviewed.
_Avoid_: Self-approval

**Independent approval**:
Authorization of a sensitive administrative change by an eligible administrator who did not request that change.
_Avoid_: Self-approval

**Patient identifier**:
A reference issued by an identity authority or insurer, separate from the clinic MRN and insufficient by itself to prove account ownership.
_Avoid_: Patient account, phone identity
