# Business Workflow Integration

Every clinic operational workflow is integrated end-to-end against `spartial_hms` with strict role separation and database-enforced state machines:

1. **Registration & Identity:** Reception creates patient (`POST /staff/patients`, gapless MRN), records encrypted NIDA/NHIF identifiers (`POST /staff/patients/:id/identifiers`), contacts, next of kin, consents, verification history, portal linkage, and runs fuzzy duplicate checks (`POST /staff/patients/duplicates/check`). Compliance merges confirmed duplicates atomically via `patient.merge_patients()`.
2. **Scheduling & Telemedicine:** Reception books appointments (`POST /staff/appointments`, GiST anti-overlap, atomic `appointment.booked` outbox enqueue). Telemedicine sessions (`POST /staff/telehealth/sessions`) require an active `telemedicine` consent and `telemedicine` appointment, and record append-only `session_event` rows.
3. **Visit, Queue, Triage & Nursing:** Reception opens encounter (`POST /staff/encounters`) and enqueues ticket (`POST /staff/queue`). Nurses record triage with auto-BMI (`POST /staff/encounters/:id/triage`), nursing observations (`POST /staff/encounters/:id/nursing`), allergies/history, and performed procedures (`POST /staff/encounters/:id/procedures`).
4. **Consultation & Orders:** Doctors author and sign immutable consultations (`POST /staff/consultations/:id/sign`), record ICD-10 diagnoses (`POST /staff/encounters/:id/diagnoses`), plan follow-ups, order diagnostic tests (`POST /staff/encounters/:id/diagnostic-requests`), and sign prescriptions (`POST /staff/encounters/:id/prescriptions`).
5. **Laboratory:** Lab technicians collect/receive/reject specimens (`SPC-...`) and enter results with automatic sex/age reference range lookup and critical-value flagging (`LL`/`HH`). Lab supervisors verify results (`POST /staff/results/:id/verify`); database enforces dual-verification unless org allows self-verification.
6. **Pharmacy:** Pharmacists receive/adjust stock batches and dispense prescriptions (`POST /staff/prescriptions/:id/dispense`, FEFO batch selection, row-locked non-negative stock ledger, idempotent) and can reverse dispenses atomically (`POST /staff/dispenses/:id/reverse`).
7. **Billing & Cash Reconciliation:** Cashiers open shifts, create draft invoices, pull unbilled procedures/drugs/lab charges via `POST /staff/billing/invoices/:id/collect-charges` (`SECURITY DEFINER` preserving clinical/billing RLS isolation), issue gapless invoices (`INV-...`), record idempotent cash/mobile-money/card/insurance payments with gapless receipts (`RCT-...`), and request refunds; billing supervisors approve four-eyes refunds, void invoices, reverse payments, and reconcile shifts.
8. **Privacy, Governance, Reporting & Worker:** Clinicians open time-bounded break-glass grants (`iam.open_break_glass`); compliance officers review break-glass (four-eyes), view per-patient PHI access logs, manage PDPA/HIPAA privacy requests, legal holds, periodic SOC 2 access reviews, dual-control approvals, and privacy alerts. Management views all 8 `reporting.v_*` views plus `staff-activity` and `staff-access`. `cmd/worker` dispatches `comms.outbox`, seals `audit.event` into `audit.seal`, and runs `gov.detect_privacy_alerts()`.
